## 2. Comprehensive Change Log: Old PPT vs. Final Rebuilt PPT

### A. What Was Removed (and Why)
1. **Fictitious Technology Stack Claims:**
   - *Removed:* Flutter, Dart, Flask, FastAPI, MySQL, Bootstrap, Scikit-Learn, PyTorch.
   - *Why:* The original draft claimed technologies that were never written into the codebase. During technical evaluation by SIH jury, presenting unverified technologies immediately fails technical cross-examination.
2. **Geographical Errors:**
   - *Removed:* Claims implying Himadri was located in Antarctica or shared Antarctic maritime logistics.
   - *Why:* Himadri is located in the Arctic (Ny-Ålesund, Svalbard, Norway at 78°55'N). Maitri and Bharati are in Antarctica. Conflating Arctic and Antarctic geography shows a lack of domain understanding to polar evaluators from NCPOR/MoES.
3. **Generic "Inventory Tracker" Mindset:**
   - *Removed:* Generic bullet points about "warehouse barcodes", "stock reordering", and "standard e-commerce logistics".
   - *Why:* Polar logistics is fundamentally different from enterprise warehousing; an undetected stockout in Antarctica is not an out-of-stock notification, it is a life-support thermal catastrophe.
4. **Vague AI Buzzwords:**
   - *Removed:* Generic mentions of "AI-powered algorithms", "machine learning models", and decorative stock art.
   - *Why:* Replaced with exact deterministic models: Causal Dependency Cascade Engine, What-If Mathematical Recalculation, and Cross-Expedition Pattern Matcher.

### B. What Was Corrected (and Why)
1. **Accurate Technical Architecture:**
   - *Corrected:* Explicitly specified React 18 SPA, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons, Leaflet interactive mapping, Recharts data visualization, Service Worker offline proxy, LocalStorage, IndexedDB, and optional Supabase replication.
   - *Why:* Matches 100% of the actual working codebase.
2. **Real Product Benchmarks & Numbers:**
   - *Corrected:* Replaced placeholder percentages with verified numbers:
     - Composite Mission Health: **63%**
     - Fuel Reserves: **58% (Amber - 18d Horizon)**
     - Power Generation: **74%**
     - Habitat Thermal: **68% (+18°C internal / -32°C outside)**
     - Science Operations: **52% (Atmospheric Lidar throttled)**
     - What-If Simulator impact: **63% $\rightarrow$ 51% health drop, 18d $\rightarrow$ 11d horizon drop, Day 12 fuel buffer breach**.
3. **Station Operational Differences:**
   - *Corrected:* Clarified that Maitri is inland rock oasis dependent on overland ice-traverses and bulk tank farms, Bharati is coastal subject to >150 km/h katabatic winds and resupplied by *MV Vasiliy Golovnin*, and Himadri is an Arctic fjord station operating under international Svalbard environmental treaties.

### C. What Was Added (and Why)
1. **The 10-Step Mission Continuity Loop:**
   - *Added:* `MONITOR -> DETECT -> ANALYZE -> PREDICT -> EXPLAIN -> SIMULATE -> RECOMMEND -> HUMAN REVIEW -> DECIDE -> UPDATE`.
   - *Why:* Establishes POLAR-AI's core USP: "Mission Continuity Intelligence" rather than a passive inventory dashboard.
2. **The Cascading Failure Propagation Engine (Slide 5):**
   - *Added:* Real benchmark tracing how a 5-day vessel delay cascades from bulk tank buffer decay into generator duty-cycle strain, habitat heat deficit, and science load shedding.
3. **What-If Scenario Simulator (Slide 6):**
   - *Added:* Interactive sandbox capability with embedded verified screenshot of our working simulator UI showing multi-variable stress testing and dynamic recommendations (`REC-001`, `REC-002`).
4. **Mission Memory Engine (Slide 7):**
   - *Added:* Learning from 6 historical Indian polar expeditions (IND-EXP-38 through IND-EXP-43) to generate preventative SOP checklists before identical environmental failures recur.
5. **Human-in-the-Loop Sovereign Governance (Slide 8):**
   - *Added:* 3-Tier confidence hierarchy (>85%, 60-85%, <60%), explicit operational tradeoff matrix (fuel conserved vs science delayed), Commander 1-click authorization buttons, and cryptographic audit log.
6. **Field-First Offline Architecture (Slide 9):**
   - *Added:* Service Worker cache-first proxy, LocalStorage & IndexedDB client persistence, and opportunistic bi-directional sync with conflict resolution.
